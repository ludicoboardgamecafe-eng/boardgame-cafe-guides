What is Kingdomino?

Kingdomino is a quick kingdom-building game. Each player starts with a tiny castle, then adds domino-shaped landscape tiles around it. The goal is to create connected territories that score points.

The game feels like classic dominoes, but instead of matching numbers, players match landscapes like fields, forests, lakes, grasslands, swamps, and mines.

Staff explanation: “You are not fighting each other directly. Everyone is building their own kingdom, but you compete for the best tiles.”

Why Guests Usually Like It

  • It looks friendly and colorful on the table.
  • Rules are simple, but players still feel clever.
  • Rounds are short, so it works well in a café setting.
  • Parents, kids, casual players, and strategy gamers can all enjoy it.
  • Scoring creates a nice “aha!” moment at the end.

Components

  • 48 domino tiles: Each tile has two landscape squares and a number on the back.
  • Starting castle tiles: One per player. This tile is wild for placement.
  • 3D castles: Placed on starting tiles, mostly for table presence.
  • King meeples: Used to reserve tiles and decide turn order.
  • Score pad: Used at the end to calculate points.
Staff tip: Before teaching, show one domino tile and one crown. Tell guests: “These crowns are the key to scoring.”

Goal of the Game

Build a kingdom inside a 5×5 grid and score the most points.

Each connected territory scores:

Number of matching connected squares × Number of crowns in that territory

Example: A forest territory with 5 connected forest squares and 2 crowns scores 5 × 2 = 10 points.

Important: A huge territory with zero crowns scores zero. Crowns are not optional; they are what make a region score.

Setup

  1. Give each player a starting tile, castle, and king meeple of the same color.
  2. Each player places their castle on their starting tile in front of them.
  3. Shuffle the domino tiles with the numbered side facing up.
  4. Use tiles depending on player count:
    • 4 players: Use all 48 tiles.
    • 3 players: Randomly remove 12 tiles. Use 36.
    • 2 players normal mode: Randomly remove 24 tiles. Use 24. Each player uses two kings.
  5. Draw tiles equal to the number of kings in play and place them in a vertical line, lowest number at the top and highest at the bottom.
  6. Flip those tiles face up.
  7. Randomly choose the first order for players to place their kings on tiles.
  8. Draw a second line of tiles, sort them by number, and flip them face up.
Easy café setup phrase: “Low number tiles are usually weaker but give earlier choice next round. High number tiles are usually stronger but make you choose later.”

How to Play

Turn order is not clockwise. It depends on where the kings are in the current tile line. The king on the top tile goes first, then the next one down, and so on.

On your turn, do these two actions in order:

  1. Place the domino you reserved last turn. Take the tile where your king is standing and add it to your kingdom.
  2. Choose a domino for next turn. Move your king to one available tile in the new line.

Placement Rules

  • The new domino must connect orthogonally, meaning side-to-side, not diagonally.
  • At least one half of the domino must touch a matching landscape type already in your kingdom.
  • Or, the domino may touch your starting castle tile. The castle is wild and accepts any landscape.
  • Your whole kingdom must fit inside an imaginary 5×5 square.
  • You may rotate the domino any way you want.
If a tile cannot be legally placed: It must be discarded. The player gets no points from that tile.

Why Turn Order Matters

After players choose tiles, the next round follows the order of the new tile line. A player who chooses the top tile will play earlier next round. A player who chooses the bottom tile will usually get a stronger tile but play later.

Scoring

At the end, score each separate territory one by one. A territory is a group of connected squares of the same terrain type.

End Game

The game ends after the last domino line has been played. Players score all territories. Highest total wins.

Tie Breaker

If there is a tie, compare the largest territory. If still tied, compare total crowns. If still tied, players share the win.

Optional Official Variants

  • Harmony: +5 bonus points if the kingdom has no gaps and no discarded dominoes.
  • Middle Kingdom: +10 bonus points if the castle is exactly in the center of the 5×5 kingdom.
  • Dynasty: Play 3 games and add scores together.
  • Mighty Duel: 2-player variant using all tiles to build a 7×7 kingdom. Best only after players know the basic game.
Café recommendation: For first-time guests, skip variants. Add Harmony and Middle Kingdom only for a second game.

Common Guest Questions

Can I rotate a tile?
Yes, any direction.
Can I connect diagonally?
No. Only side-to-side connections count.
Can I connect any terrain to the castle?
Yes. The starting castle tile is wild.
Can my kingdom be 6×4?
No. It must fit inside a 5×5 square.
Can I leave holes?
Yes, but holes may make later placement harder. No penalty unless using Harmony variant.
Do crowns score alone?
No. Territory size multiplied by crowns gives points.
Do two same terrains count as one region if they touch diagonally?
No. Diagonal touching does not connect territories.
Can I discard a tile even if I can place it?
Usually no. If it can be legally placed, place it. Discard only when it cannot legally fit.

Common Mistakes to Watch For

  • Players forget the 5×5 limit and build too wide.
  • Players score a no-crown territory by mistake.
  • Players combine separate same-terrain areas that are not connected.
  • Players think turn order is clockwise.
  • Players forget to move their king to a tile for the next round.
  • Players place tiles diagonally and think that counts as connected.

2–3 Minute Teaching Script

“In Kingdomino, everyone is building their own 5×5 kingdom. Each turn, you place the tile your king is on, then choose a tile for next round. When placing, at least one side must touch a matching terrain, or it can touch your castle because the castle is wild. At the end, each connected territory scores its size multiplied by the crowns inside it. So a big area with no crowns is zero, but a big area with crowns is great. The tricky part is choosing tiles: stronger tiles are lower in the line, but choosing them means you may play later next round.”

Staff Strategy Tips for Helping Guests

  • Tell new players not to chase only big areas. They need crowns.
  • Encourage them to keep space open near the castle in the early game.
  • Remind them that mines often score well because they can have many crowns, even if the area is small.
  • When a guest is stuck, ask: “Which side can match, and will this still fit in 5×5?”
  • For kids, focus only on matching terrain and crowns. Strategy can come later.
Best staff habit: During the first two rounds, quietly check every player’s 5×5 boundary. This prevents frustration near the end.

Café Staff Hosting Notes

  • Best table size: Small to medium table. Each player needs space for a 5×5 grid.
  • Best audience: Families, couples, first-time gamers, casual groups.
  • When to recommend: “Do you want something quick, pretty, and strategic but not too heavy?”
  • When not to recommend: If guests want party games, loud social deduction, or high interaction.
  • Reset tip: Sort nothing after play; just shuffle tiles numbered-side up and remove based on player count.

Kingdomino എന്താണ്?

Kingdomino ഒരു quick kingdom-building game ആണ്. ഓരോ player-നും ഒരു ചെറിയ castle-ൽ നിന്ന് തുടങ്ങും. അതിനുചുറ്റും domino shape ഉള്ള landscape tiles വെച്ച് സ്വന്തം kingdom build ചെയ്യും.

Classic dominoes പോലെ തോന്നും, പക്ഷേ numbers match ചെയ്യുന്നതിന് പകരം ഇവിടെ landscapes ആണ് match ചെയ്യുന്നത്: fields, forests, lakes, grasslands, swamps, mines.

സ്റ്റാഫ് explanation: “ഇത് direct fighting game അല്ല. എല്ലാവരും സ്വന്തം kingdom build ചെയ്യുന്നു, പക്ഷേ best tiles എടുക്കാൻ compete ചെയ്യും.”

Guests-ന് ഇത് ഇഷ്ടപ്പെടാനുള്ള കാരണങ്ങൾ

  • Table-ൽ colorful ആയി കാണാം.
  • Rules simple ആണ്, പക്ഷേ നല്ല strategy ഉണ്ട്.
  • Short game ആണ്, café setting-ന് perfect.
  • Kids, family, casual players, gamers എല്ലാർക്കും പറ്റും.
  • End scoring സമയത്ത് നല്ല excitement ഉണ്ടാകും.

Components

  • 48 domino tiles: ഓരോ tile-ലും രണ്ട് landscape squares ഉണ്ട്.
  • Starting castle tiles: ഓരോ player-നും ഒന്ന്. Placement സമയത്ത് ഇത് wild tile ആണ്.
  • 3D castles: Starting tile-ൽ വെക്കും.
  • King meeples: Tiles reserve ചെയ്യാനും turn order കാണിക്കാനും.
  • Score pad: അവസാനം score calculate ചെയ്യാൻ.
Staff tip: Teaching തുടങ്ങുമ്പോൾ ഒരു tile-ും crown-ും കാണിച്ച് പറയുക: “ഈ crowns ആണ് scoring-ൽ main.”

Game Objective

5×5 grid-ന്റെ ഉള്ളിൽ ഏറ്റവും കൂടുതൽ points കിട്ടുന്ന kingdom build ചെയ്യുക.

ഓരോ connected territory-യും ഇങ്ങനെ score ചെയ്യും:

Matching connected squares-ന്റെ എണ്ണം × ആ territory-യിലെ crowns

Example: 5 connected forest squares ഉണ്ട്, അതിൽ 2 crowns ഉണ്ട് എങ്കിൽ score 5 × 2 = 10 points.

Important: വലിയ territory ആയാലും crowns ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ score zero ആണ്.

Setup

  1. ഓരോ player-നും same colour starting tile, castle, king meeple കൊടുക്കുക.
  2. Player castle starting tile-ൽ വെക്കണം.
  3. Domino tiles numbered side up ആയി shuffle ചെയ്യുക.
  4. Player count അനുസരിച്ച് tiles ഉപയോഗിക്കുക:
    • 4 players: 48 tiles എല്ലാം ഉപയോഗിക്കുക.
    • 3 players: Random ആയി 12 tiles remove ചെയ്യുക. 36 ഉപയോഗിക്കുക.
    • 2 players normal mode: Random ആയി 24 tiles remove ചെയ്യുക. 24 ഉപയോഗിക്കുക. ഓരോ player-നും രണ്ട് kings.
  5. Play-ലുള്ള kings എണ്ണത്തിന് തുല്യമായി tiles draw ചെയ്ത് vertical line ആയി വെക്കുക. Lowest number top-ൽ, highest number bottom-ൽ.
  6. Tiles face up ആക്കുക.
  7. First round-ൽ players random order-ൽ kings place ചെയ്യുക.
  8. അടുത്ത line tiles draw ചെയ്ത് number order-ൽ arrange ചെയ്ത് face up ആക്കുക.
Easy café line: “Low number tile എടുത്താൽ next round early choice കിട്ടും. High number tile ശക്തമായിരിക്കും, പക്ഷേ next round late choice ആകാം.”

എങ്ങനെ കളിക്കാം?

Turn order clockwise അല്ല. Current tile line-ൽ kings ഏത് order-ൽ നിൽക്കുന്നു എന്നതനുസരിച്ചാണ്. Top tile-ൽ king ഉള്ള player ആദ്യം, പിന്നെ താഴേക്ക്.

നിങ്ങളുടെ turn-ൽ രണ്ട് കാര്യങ്ങൾ:

  1. Last turn reserve ചെയ്ത domino place ചെയ്യുക. King നിൽക്കുന്ന tile എടുത്ത് kingdom-ൽ ചേർക്കുക.
  2. Next turn-നുള്ള domino choose ചെയ്യുക. New line-ൽ free tile-ിൽ king വെക്കുക.

Placement Rules

  • Domino side-to-side connect ചെയ്യണം. Diagonal count ആവില്ല.
  • Domino-യുടെ കുറഞ്ഞത് ഒരു half എങ്കിലും kingdom-ൽ already ഉള്ള matching landscape-നോട് touch ചെയ്യണം.
  • അല്ലെങ്കിൽ starting castle tile-നോട് touch ചെയ്യാം. Castle wild ആണ്.
  • മുഴുവൻ kingdom ഒരു imaginary 5×5 square-ന്റെ ഉള്ളിൽ fit ആകണം.
  • Domino rotate ചെയ്യാം.
Tile legally place ചെയ്യാൻ പറ്റിയില്ലെങ്കിൽ: അത് discard ചെയ്യണം. ആ tile points തരില്ല.

Turn Order എന്തുകൊണ്ട് important?
