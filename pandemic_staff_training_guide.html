What is Pandemic?

Pandemic is a cooperative board game. That means all players are on the same team. They either save the world together or lose together.

Players are disease-fighting specialists travelling around the world, treating diseases, building research stations, sharing city cards, and discovering cures for four diseases before things get out of control.

Players: 2-4
Time: About 45 minutes
Difficulty: Medium for new players
Best for: Guests who enjoy teamwork and planning
Staff angle: Tell guests this is not a competitive game. The fun comes from discussing plans, managing crisis, and celebrating clever teamwork.

Theme in One Line

Four diseases are spreading across the world, and the players are a specialist team trying to cure all four before the world collapses.

Keep the tone exciting but not scary. For cafe teaching, say: “It’s like a team puzzle where the board keeps fighting back.”

Main Goal

The team wins immediately when all four cures have been discovered.

Important: Players do not need to remove every disease cube from the board to win. Curing all four diseases is enough.

Components Staff Should Know

  • Game board with world map and connected cities
  • 4 disease cube colors: blue, yellow, black, red
  • Player pawns and role cards
  • Player cards: city cards, event cards, epidemic cards
  • Infection cards
  • Research stations
  • Cure markers
  • Infection rate marker
  • Outbreak marker
  • Reference cards
Staff tip: Keep player cards and infection cards clearly separate. New players mix these up very often.

Setup: Step by Step

  1. Place the board in the center.
  2. Put one research station in Atlanta.
  3. Place all pawns in Atlanta.
  4. Place cure markers near the cure track, vial side up / uncured side.
  5. Put infection rate marker on the first space of the infection rate track.
  6. Put outbreak marker on 0.
  7. Shuffle infection cards. Infect 9 cities: first 3 cities get 3 cubes each, next 3 get 2 cubes each, next 3 get 1 cube each.
  8. Give each player a random role card and matching pawn.
  9. Deal player cards: 2 players get 4 cards each, 3 players get 3 each, 4 players get 2 each.
  10. Choose difficulty by adding epidemic cards to the player deck: usually 4 for introductory, 5 for standard, 6 for heroic.
  11. Split player deck into equal piles, shuffle one epidemic into each pile, then stack piles to form the player draw deck.
Cafe recommendation: For first-time guests, use 4 epidemic cards. For experienced players, use 5.

Turn Structure

Each player's turn has exactly three main parts:

  1. Do 4 actions
  2. Draw 2 player cards
  3. Infect cities
Simple explanation: “On your turn, you get 4 actions to help the team. Then you draw 2 cards. Then the disease spreads.”

The 4 Actions: What Can Players Do?

Players may do any mix of these actions. They can repeat the same action multiple times.

Movement Actions

ActionHow it worksSimple cafe explanation
Drive / FerryMove along one white line to a connected city.Move to a neighbouring city.
Direct FlightDiscard a city card to move to that city.Play a city card and fly there.
Charter FlightDiscard the card matching your current city to move anywhere.Use your current city card as a ticket to anywhere.
Shuttle FlightMove from one research station to another research station.Station-to-station travel.

Other Important Actions

ActionHow it worksStaff note

Common correction: Share Knowledge usually only works for the city card of the city both players are standing in. The Researcher has a special exception when giving cards.

Player Cards and Hand Limit

After actions, the player draws 2 player cards.

  • City cards help with flights, sharing, building stations, and discovering cures.
  • Event cards are powerful one-time abilities.
  • Epidemic cards trigger trouble immediately and are not kept in hand.

The hand limit is 7 cards. If a player has more than 7, they must immediately discard down to 7 or play event cards to reduce hand size.

Staff reminder: Event cards do not cost an action and can usually be played on any player's turn unless the card says otherwise.

Epidemic Cards: The Big Trouble

When an epidemic is drawn, follow three steps:

  1. Increase: Move the infection rate marker forward one space.
  2. Infect: Draw the bottom infection card and add 3 cubes to that city.
  3. Intensify: Shuffle the infection discard pile and place it back on top of the infection deck.
Why this matters: Cities that were recently infected are likely to appear again soon. This creates the pressure and drama of Pandemic.

Infecting Cities

At the end of each turn, draw infection cards equal to the current infection rate. For each card, add 1 cube of that city's disease color to that city.

If the city already has 3 cubes of that color, do not add a fourth cube. Instead, an outbreak happens.

Outbreaks

An outbreak happens when a city would receive a 4th cube of the same color.

  1. Move the outbreak marker up by 1.
  2. Add 1 cube of that disease color to every connected city.
  3. If those cities also already have 3 cubes, chain outbreaks can happen.
Important edge case: In one chain reaction, the same city cannot outbreak more than once.

Cured vs Eradicated

TermMeaning
CuredPlayers discovered the cure. Treat Disease removes all cubes of that color from a city instead of just one.
EradicatedThe disease is cured and there are no cubes of that color anywhere on the board. Future infection cards of that color do nothing.
Simple line: Cure means we know the medicine. Eradicated means the disease is completely gone from the board.

How Players Win or Lose

Win

The team wins immediately when all 4 diseases are cured.

Lose

  • The 8th outbreak happens.
  • Players need to add a disease cube but that color's supply is empty.
  • A player needs to draw player cards but there are not enough cards in the player deck.

Roles: How to Explain Them

Roles give players special powers. Staff do not need to explain every role before the game starts. Explain each player's role after dealing it.

RoleSimple explanation
MedicRemoves all cubes of one color when treating. Very good at cleanup.
ScientistNeeds only 4 cards of one color to cure instead of 5.
ResearcherCan give any city card when sharing knowledge.
DispatcherCan move other players' pawns with permission.
Operations ExpertCan build a research station without discarding the matching city card.
Quarantine SpecialistPrevents cubes and outbreaks in her city and connected cities.
Contingency PlannerCan save an event card for later use.

Common Guest Questions

Can we show our cards?
Rules say players may openly discuss strategy, but should not reveal exact cards in standard play. In casual cafe play with beginners, many groups play more openly. Staff can say: “Officially, discuss but do not fully show cards. For your first game, be relaxed if everyone agrees.”

Do we win after curing one disease?
No. You need all four cures.

Do we need to clear all cubes?
No. The win is instant when the fourth cure is discovered.

Can I use fewer than 4 actions?
Yes, but usually players should use all 4 if possible.

Can event cards be played anytime?
Usually yes, and they do not cost an action. Follow card text.

Can one player tell everyone what to do?
Technically the group can discuss, but staff should encourage everyone to speak so one person does not dominate.

Common Mistakes Staff Should Watch For

  • Players forget to infect cities after drawing player cards.
  • Players add a 4th cube instead of causing an outbreak.
  • Players share any card, even when neither is the Researcher.
  • Players think curing a disease removes all cubes immediately.
  • Players forget the 7-card hand limit.
  • Players forget to shuffle infection discard and place it on top during epidemic.
  • Players try to discover a cure without being at a research station.

Good Staff Facilitation Tips

  • Teach the goal first: cure all four diseases.
  • Teach turn structure second: 4 actions, draw 2, infect.
  • Do not explain every role in detail upfront. Explain only the roles in play.
  • Warn guests that the game is hard and losing is normal.
  • Encourage quieter players to suggest plans.
  • For first-timers, recommend introductory difficulty with 4 epidemic cards.

3-Minute Staff Teaching Script

“Pandemic is a cooperative game, so all of you are one team. Your goal is to discover cures for all four diseases before the world gets overwhelmed.”

“On your turn, you get 4 actions. You can move, treat disease cubes, build research stations, share cards, or discover a cure. After that, you draw 2 player cards, then the disease spreads by drawing infection cards.”

“The main thing you are trying to do is collect 5 cards of the same color and go to a research station to cure that color. Once all four colors are cured, you win immediately.”

“But epidemics make infected cities come back again, and outbreaks happen if a city would get a fourth cube. So you need to balance curing diseases with controlling the board.”

  • Cure ചെയ്താൽ disease cubes എല്ലാം ഉടൻ പോകും എന്ന് കരുതുന്നു.
  • 7-card hand limit മറക്കുന്നു.
  • Epidemic intensify step-ൽ discard pile top-ൽ വെക്കാൻ മറക്കുന്നു.
  • Research station ഇല്ലാതെ cure ചെയ്യാൻ ശ്രമിക്കുന്നു.

Staff Facilitation Tips

  • ആദ്യം goal explain ചെയ്യുക: four cures.
  • പിന്നെ turn structure: 4 actions, draw 2, infect.
  • എല്ലാ roles ഒരുമിച്ച് explain ചെയ്യേണ്ട.
  • Game hard ആണെന്നും losing normal ആണെന്നും പറയുക.
  • Quiet players-നെയും discussion-ൽ involve ചെയ്യുക.
  • First-timers-ന് 4 epidemic cards recommend ചെയ്യുക.
