Downforce Staff Training Guide

For board game cafe staff in Kochi | English + Malayalam

1. What is Downforce?

Downforce is a fast racing board game where players are not just drivers. They are also team owners, card players, and gamblers. Players first bid to own cars, then use cards to move all cars around the race track, and during the race they secretly bet on which car they think will win.

The fun twist is that your cards often move other players' cars too. So you are always asking: “Should I help my own car, block someone else, or make a smart bet on a car that is already doing well?”

Staff positioning: Tell guests it feels like Formula 1 mixed with poker-style mind games. It is easy to learn, but the table talk and clever moves make it exciting.

3. Components

  • 1 double-sided race board
  • 6 race cars in different colours
  • 48 speed cards
  • 6 special power cards
  • 6 driver plaques
  • Betting / score sheets
  • Pencil or pen for each player
Staff check before giving the game: Make sure the score sheets and pencils are available. Downforce needs writing for bids and bets.

4. The Big Idea: How Do You Win?

Players earn money from three things:

  1. Race prize money from the cars they own.
  2. Betting money if their secret bets finish in the top positions.
  3. Saving money by not overpaying during the auction.

At the end:

Total winnings = race prize money + successful bets − auction money spent

Important staff line: “This is not only about making your car win. You can win by buying cheap, betting smart, and moving the right cars at the right time.”

5. Setup

  1. Place the race board in the middle of the table.
  2. Put all 6 cars on the starting spaces.
  3. Shuffle the speed cards and deal them to players. In some player counts, leftover cards are returned to the box.
  4. Separate the special Speed-8 cards if your edition uses them for auction setup.
  5. Shuffle the power cards.
  6. Give each player a score sheet and pencil.
  7. Keep driver plaques near the board.
Teaching shortcut: Do not explain every power card before the game. Explain that each power bends one rule, then explain individual powers when players receive them.

6. Game Structure

Downforce has three connected parts:

PartWhat happens?Why it matters
1. AuctionPlayers bid for cars and powers.Buying too expensive can lose the game.
2. RacePlayers play cards to move cars.Cards move multiple cars, not only your own.
3. BettingPlayers secretly bet during the race.Smart bets can beat race prize money.

7. Auction Phase Explained

Before the race starts, the cars are auctioned one by one. Each auction usually shows:

  • One car colour
  • One special power

Every player secretly chooses one card from hand and reveals it at the same time. The number on that card matching the auctioned car colour is that player's bid.

The highest bid wins the car, the driver plaque, and the power card. The winning player records the bid amount on the score sheet. This money is not paid with tokens now; it is subtracted from final earnings.

Tie in auction

If players bid the same amount, check the official tie rules for your edition. Usually, the card with more car symbols wins; if still tied, the card without the wild symbol wins.

Example: Red car is being auctioned. Kevin reveals a card with Red 5. Anu reveals Red 3. Kevin wins Red for $5M and writes $5M as a cost.
Common guest confusion: Players do not lose the card they bid with. Bid cards return to the players' hands after each auction.

8. Race Phase: Playing Speed Cards

On your turn, play one speed card from your hand. The card lists car colours and movement numbers.

Move the cars in the order shown on the card, from top to bottom, unless a power changes this.

  • You move every car shown on the card.
  • You may have to move opponent cars.
  • You may even move cars that nobody owns.
  • After resolving the card, discard it.
Example: A card says Blue 6, Orange 4, Yellow 1. Move Blue 6 first, then Orange 4, then Yellow 1.
Staff phrase: “Your cards are like race commands. They push several cars forward, so every card helps someone and hurts someone.”

9. Wild Cars on Cards

Some cards show a white/wild car. The player who played the card chooses which car colour the wild represents.

  • The wild cannot normally copy a colour already printed on that same card.
  • If there are two wilds, they must normally be two different colours.
  • You can choose a car even if it cannot move, but that may waste the movement.
Powers can change wild rules. If a power card says something different, the power card wins.

10. Movement Rules

Cars move forward space by space. A car can move straight forward or diagonally forward, depending on the track space.

  • Cars cannot move sideways or backwards.
  • Cars cannot move through other cars.
  • If a car has a legal move, it must move.
  • If it cannot move the full number, move it as far as possible.
  • You may choose a route that causes the car to get blocked later, as long as each step is legal.
Staff demonstration: When teaching, physically move one car diagonally and one car straight. Guests understand movement much faster by seeing it once.

11. Blocking

Blocking is a major part of Downforce. Because cars cannot pass through other cars, a player may move a car into a tight lane to slow down cars behind it.

Blocking is legal as long as the move itself is legal.

Example: If Black is behind Red and the lane is narrow, moving Red only a few spaces may stop Black from using its full movement later.
Do not let guests move a car through an occupied space. Even if the card says 6, the car may only move 2 or 3 if blocked.

12. Betting During the Race

There are three betting lines on the track. When the first car crosses a betting line for the first time, finish resolving the entire card first. Then every player secretly writes a bet on their sheet.

  • Players can bet on their own car.
  • Players can bet on someone else's car.
  • Players can bet on the same car multiple times across betting rounds.
  • Bets pay based on whether that car finishes 1st, 2nd, or 3rd.
Staff reminder: Betting happens after the full card is resolved, not immediately when a car crosses the line mid-card.

13. Finishing the Race

When a car crosses the finish line, place it in the appropriate finishing position area: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and so on.

  • Extra movement after crossing the finish line does not matter.
  • Once a car finishes, it does not move again.
  • If all cars owned by a player have finished, that player discards the rest of their cards and stops playing cards.
  • Some cars may not finish if the race ends before they cross the line.

14. Scoring

At the end, players calculate:

  1. Money earned from owned cars based on finishing position.
  2. Money earned from successful bets.
  3. Subtract total auction bid amounts.

The player with the most money wins.

Example: A player earns $18M from race prizes and $9M from bets. They spent $7M in the auction. Final score = 18 + 9 − 7 = $20M.

Tie-breaker

If players are tied, the tied player whose car finished highest wins the tie. If needed, check the exact edition rulebook for final tie resolution.

15. Power Cards - Staff Overview

Power cards give special abilities. They are easy to forget, so remind guests to keep them visible.

PowerSimple explanationStaff note
AggressiveYour car may get extra movement when it appears first on a card.Good for pushing your car ahead.
CunningYou control movement of your cars even on other players' turns.Owner must remember to use it.
DeterminedYour car may get bonus movement in certain conditions.Check the card text carefully.
StrategicYou may ignore one car on a card you play.Great for avoiding helping rivals.
TrickyYou can change the order of car movement on your card.Very strong for blocking.
UnpredictableWild choices become more flexible.Useful for surprise moves.
Power cards override normal rules when they conflict. Always read the exact power card text aloud if there is doubt.

16. 3-Minute Staff Teaching Script

“Downforce is a racing game, but you are also betting on the race. First, we auction the cars. Try not to overpay, because your bid is subtracted at the end. Then we race by playing cards. Each card moves multiple cars, so you may move your own car and other players' cars too. During the race, when a car crosses each betting line, everyone secretly bets on who they think will finish first. At the end you earn money from your cars, your bets, and then subtract what you spent in the auction. Most money wins. So remember: buy smart, race smart, bet smart.”

17. How to Help Guests During Play

  • During auction, remind them: “High bid wins, but expensive cars can hurt your final score.”
  • During racing, ask: “Are you sure you want to move that opponent car that far?”
  • At betting lines, pause the game and make everyone write secretly.
  • When guests argue about movement, check: forward only, no sideways, no passing through cars.
  • For new groups, explain that blocking is legal and part of the fun.

18. Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting to subtract auction bids at the end.
  • Thinking only car owners can move their cars.
  • Betting immediately when a car crosses a line before finishing the whole card.
  • Moving cars sideways or through other cars.
  • Forgetting power cards.
  • Assuming the race winner always wins the game.

19. Common Guest Questions

Can I move another player's car?
Yes. If it is on the card you played, you move it unless a power says otherwise.
Can I choose not to move a car?
Normally no. If the car has a legal move, it must move. Some powers can change this.
Can I win without owning the winning car?
Yes. Good bets and cheap auction prices can win the game.
Can I bet on the same car more than once?
Yes.
Do I reveal my bets?
No. Keep them secret until final scoring.
What happens if a car cannot move full spaces?
Move it as far as legally possible.
Can cars pass through each other?
No.
What if a car crosses the betting line in the middle of a card?
Finish all movements on that card first. Then everyone bets.

20. Staff Recommendation

Downforce is great for groups who want something competitive, loud, and interactive but not too complex. It works especially well for friends, families with older kids, and mixed groups where some people are new to board games.

Do not recommend first to guests who dislike direct competition, blocking, or betting-style decisions.

7. Auction Phase

Race തുടങ്ങുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് ഓരോ car-വും auction ചെയ്യും. ഒരു car colour ഉം ഒരു power card ഉം കാണിക്കും.

ഓരോ player-യും കൈയിലെ ഒരു card secret ആയി choose ചെയ്ത് reveal ചെയ്യും. ആ card-ൽ ആ car colour-ന്റെ number ആണ് bid amount.

Highest bid ചെയ്ത player car-ഉം power-ഉം നേടും. Bid amount score sheet-ൽ cost ആയി എഴുതണം.

Example: Red car auction ആണ്. Rahul-ന്റെ card-ൽ Red 5. Neha-യുടെ card-ൽ Red 3. Rahul Red car $5M-ന് വാങ്ങും.
Bid ചെയ്യാൻ ഉപയോഗിച്ച card കളയുന്നില്ല. അത് player-ന്റെ hand-ലേക്ക് തിരികെ പോകും.

8. Race Phase - Speed Cards

നിങ്ങളുടെ turn-ൽ ഒരു speed card play ചെയ്യുക. Card-ൽ car colours ഉം movement numbers ഉം കാണും.

  • Card-ൽ കാണുന്ന cars എല്ലാം move ചെയ്യണം.
  • നിങ്ങളുടെ car മാത്രം അല്ല, opponent car-ഉം move ചെയ്യേണ്ടി വരാം.
  • Card-ൽ മുകളിൽ നിന്ന് താഴേക്ക് order പാലിക്കുക.
  • Card resolve ചെയ്ത ശേഷം discard ചെയ്യുക.
Example: Blue 6, Orange 4, Yellow 1 എന്ന് card പറയുന്നു. ആദ്യം Blue 6, പിന്നെ Orange 4, പിന്നെ Yellow 1 move ചെയ്യുക.

9. Wild Cars

ചില cards-ൽ white/wild car ഉണ്ടാകും. അത് ഏത് colour car ആയി ഉപയോഗിക്കണമെന്ന് card കളിച്ച player തീരുമാനിക്കും.

  • സാധാരണയായി card-ൽ already printed colour wild ആയി choose ചെയ്യാൻ പാടില്ല.
  • രണ്ട് wild ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ രണ്ട് different colours വേണം.
  • Power card rules ഇത് മാറ്റാം.

10. Movement Rules

  • Cars forward മാത്രം move ചെയ്യും.
  • Straight forward അല്ലെങ്കിൽ diagonal forward move ചെയ്യാം.
  • Sideways / backwards move ചെയ്യാൻ പാടില്ല.
  • Cars മറ്റൊരു car-ന്റെ through move ചെയ്യാൻ പാടില്ല.
  • Full movement സാധ്യമല്ലെങ്കിൽ കഴിയുന്നത്ര move ചെയ്യുക.
  • Legal move ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ car move ചെയ്യണം.
Staff tip: Movement explain ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ board-ൽ ഒന്ന് കാണിച്ച് കൊടുത്താൽ guests വേഗം മനസ്സിലാക്കും.

11. Blocking

Blocking ഈ game-ന്റെ പ്രധാന fun ആണ്. Cars pass ചെയ്യാൻ കഴിയാത്തതിനാൽ, ഒരു car tight lane-ൽ നിർത്തി പിന്നിലെ cars slow ചെയ്യാം.

Move legal ആണെങ്കിൽ blocking allowed ആണ്.

12. Betting

Track-ൽ മൂന്ന് betting lines ഉണ്ടാകും. ആദ്യ car ഒരു betting line cross ചെയ്താൽ, ആദ്യം ആ card-ലെ മുഴുവൻ movement പൂർത്തിയാക്കുക. പിന്നെ എല്ലാവരും secret bet എഴുതുക.

  • സ്വന്തം car-ൽ bet ചെയ്യാം.
  • മറ്റുള്ളവരുടെ car-ൽ bet ചെയ്യാം.
  • ഒരേ car-ൽ പല betting rounds-ലും bet ചെയ്യാം.
  • Bets race result അനുസരിച്ച് money നൽകും.
Car line cross ചെയ്ത ഉടനെ bet ചെയ്യരുത്. ആ card മുഴുവൻ resolve ചെയ്ത ശേഷം bet ചെയ്യണം.

13. Race Finish

  • Car finish line cross ചെയ്താൽ finishing position area-ൽ വയ്ക്കുക.
  • Finish line കഴിഞ്ഞ extra movement കാര്യമില്ല.
  • Finish ചെയ്ത car ഇനി move ചെയ്യില്ല.
  • ഒരു player-ന്റെ എല്ലാ cars finish ചെയ്താൽ അവർ ബാക്കി cards discard ചെയ്യും.

14. Scoring

  1. Owned cars-ന്റെ race prize കണക്കാക്കുക.
  2. Correct bets-ൽ നിന്ന് കിട്ടിയ money ചേർക്കുക.
  3. Auction-ൽ ചെലവാക്കിയ bid amount കുറയ്ക്കുക.

ഏറ്റവും കൂടുതൽ money ഉള്ള player ജയിക്കും.

Example: Race prize $18M, bets $9M, auction cost $7M. Final = 18 + 9 − 7 = $20M.
