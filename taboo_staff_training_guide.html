What is Taboo?

Taboo is a party game where one player tries to make their team guess a secret word, but they cannot say the obvious related words listed on the card.

Example: the guess word might be “Pizza”, but the clue-giver may not be allowed to say words like cheese, crust, slice, tomato, Italian. So they must get creative: “This is something you order for a birthday party, usually round, and people fight for the last piece.”

Staff angle: Sell this as “a fast shouting-and-laughing word game where your brain keeps trying to say the words you are not allowed to say.”

Components

  • Taboo cards: Each card has one guess word and a list of forbidden Taboo words.
  • Timer: Usually a sand timer or app timer.
  • Buzzer / squeaker: Used by the opposite team when the clue-giver breaks a rule.
  • Score pad: To track team points.
  • Card holder: Some versions include a stand/easel.

Different editions have slightly different components, but the core gameplay is the same.

Setup

  1. Split players into two teams. Try to balance confident speakers across both teams.
  2. Place the deck where the clue-giver can draw cards easily.
  3. Keep the timer and buzzer ready.
  4. Choose one team to start.
  5. From the starting team, choose one player as the clue-giver.
  6. The other team should assign one or more players to watch the card and hold the buzzer.
Cafe tip: For mixed-language groups in Kochi, ask before starting: “English clues only, Malayalam allowed, or both okay?” Decide clearly so no one argues mid-game.

How to Play

1. Start the timer

The clue-giver picks up the first card. Their job is to make their own team guess the main guess word.

2. Give clues without saying forbidden words

The clue-giver can describe, explain, joke, compare, or give situational hints. But they cannot say the guess word or any Taboo word shown on the card.

3. Team guesses loudly

The clue-giver’s teammates shout guesses. There is no turn order inside the team.

4. Correct guess = score pile

If the team guesses correctly, keep that card as a point for the clue-giver’s team and immediately move to the next card.

5. Rule break = buzzer

If the clue-giver says a forbidden word or breaks a clue rule, the opposite team buzzes. That card is dead and usually counts for the opposite team.

6. Skip if stuck

The clue-giver may skip a card if they are stuck. Depending on your house rule or edition, skipped cards may count against the team or go to the opposite team. Pick one rule before the game starts.

7. Time ends

When time runs out, stop immediately. Count successful cards and penalty cards, then the next team takes a turn.

Important Clue Rules

Do not say the guess word. Even part of the word is usually not allowed. For “football,” saying “foot” or “ball” is not okay.
Do not say any Taboo word. If the forbidden word is “doctor,” saying “doctors” or “doctoring” is also not okay.
No gestures or acting. This is not charades. No pointing, miming, or hand signals.
No sound effects used as direct clues. For example, for “dog,” barking like a dog should not be allowed.
No spelling or rhyming tricks. Do not say “starts with P,” “rhymes with cake,” or spell letters.
No foreign-language loopholes. If the group decides English-only, do not translate forbidden words into Malayalam/Hindi to sneak around the card. If bilingual play is allowed, staff should clarify that translated forbidden words are also banned.
Allowed style: “You use this in the kitchen to make food hot.”
Not allowed: Pointing to the cafe oven, making microwave sounds, or saying a forbidden listed word.

Scoring

What happened?Who gets the point?
Your team guessed the word correctlyYour team gets 1 point
You said a Taboo word or broke a clue ruleOpposite team usually gets 1 point
You skipped a cardUse your chosen house rule: no point, or opposite team gets 1 point

After each team has had the same number of turns, the team with more points wins.

Recommended cafe house rule: For casual guests, skipped cards give no point to either team. For competitive groups, skipped cards give 1 point to the opposite team.

How Long Should We Play?

  • Quick cafe round: Each player gets one turn as clue-giver.
  • Standard game: Play until each player has given clues twice.
  • Large group: Play for a fixed time, like 20 minutes.
  • Table waiting game: Play 3 rounds per team and declare winner.

Common Mistakes New Players Make

  • Accidentally saying part of the guess word.
  • Using gestures without realizing it.
  • Reading the Taboo words aloud by mistake.
  • Arguing after buzzer instead of moving quickly.
  • Giving clues that are too long and slow.
  • Forgetting that plural/singular forms count as the same word.

Staff Tips for Running Taboo in the Cafe

  • Keep the energy high. Taboo is better when people are laughing and guessing fast.
  • Before starting, clearly say what languages are allowed.
  • If a group has kids, remove adult/difficult cards if needed.
  • For shy players, let them guess first before becoming clue-giver.
  • Use phone timer if the sand timer is missing.
  • If the buzzer is too annoying for nearby tables, ask them to tap the table or say “buzz” instead.

Common Guest Questions

Can I say a word that sounds similar?

No, not as a direct trick. Rhyming and sound clues are usually not allowed.

Can I use Malayalam clues?

Yes if the group agrees before starting. But translated versions of the guess word or Taboo words should still be banned.

Can I say initials or first letter?

No. Letter clues, spelling, and “starts with” hints are not allowed.

Can I pass?

Yes, but decide the skip penalty before starting.

What if the opposite team buzzes wrongly?

For casual play, just continue. For strict play, staff can say the card stays alive if no rule was broken.

Can teammates ask questions?

They can shout guesses, but the clue-giver should only respond with more clues, not “yes/no” confirmation unless your group allows it.

30-Second Teaching Script

“Taboo is a team word game. One person gives clues so their team guesses the word on the card. But below the word there are forbidden words you cannot say. If you say one, the other team buzzes you. Correct guesses score points. Try to get through as many cards as possible before the timer ends.”

2-Minute Teaching Script

“Split into two teams. On your team’s turn, one player becomes the clue-giver. They pick a card and try to make their team guess the top word. The tricky part is that they cannot say the listed Taboo words, the guess word, parts of the word, spelling hints, rhymes, gestures, or sound effects. The opposite team watches the card and buzzes if a rule is broken. Correct cards go to your team’s score pile. Buzzer cards go to the other team or penalty pile, depending on the rule we choose. When time runs out, count points and swap teams.”

Staff angle: Guests-നോട് പറയാം: “നിങ്ങൾ പറയാൻ ഏറ്റവും തോന്നുന്ന words തന്നെ പറയാൻ പാടില്ലാത്ത funny guessing game.”

Components

  • Taboo cards: ഓരോ card-ലും guess word + forbidden words.
  • Timer: Sand timer അല്ലെങ്കിൽ phone timer.

Setup

  1. Players-നെ 2 teams ആയി split ചെയ്യുക.
  2. Card deck clue-giver എളുപ്പത്തിൽ എടുക്കാൻ പറ്റുന്നിടത്ത് വെക്കുക.
  3. Timer and buzzer ready ആക്കുക.
  4. ഏത് team തുടങ്ങും എന്ന് തീരുമാനിക്കുക.
  5. Starting team-ൽ നിന്ന് ഒരാളെ clue-giver ആക്കുക.
  6. Opposite team card നോക്കി buzzer പിടിക്കും.
Cafe tip: Kochi groups-ൽ game തുടങ്ങുന്നതിന് മുൻപ് ചോദിക്കുക: “English only ആണോ, Malayalam allowed ആണോ, രണ്ടും okay ആണോ?”

എങ്ങനെ കളിക്കാം?

1. Timer start ചെയ്യുക

Clue-giver ആദ്യ card എടുക്കും. സ്വന്തം team guess word പറയണം.

2. Forbidden words പറയാതെ clues കൊടുക്കുക

3. Team guesses പറയും

Team members ഒരുമിച്ച് guesses shout ചെയ്യാം. Team-ൽ turn order ഇല്ല.

4. Correct guess = point

Team ശരിയായി പറഞ്ഞാൽ card score pile-ൽ വെച്ച് next card എടുക്കുക.

5. Rule break = buzzer

6. Stuck ആണെങ്കിൽ skip ചെയ്യാം
