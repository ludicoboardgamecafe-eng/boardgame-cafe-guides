Board Game Cafe Staff Guide: Coup

Coup

A quick bluffing and deduction card game. Everyone has two hidden character cards, but players can claim almost any power. The fun is in lying confidently, catching lies, and surviving until everyone else loses their influence.

Bluffing game2-6 playersAbout 15 minutesEasy rules, spicy choicesGreat for cafe groups

1. What is the game?

Coup is a small-card social bluffing game. Each player starts with two face-down character cards. These cards are called your influence. If both your cards are revealed, you are out.

On your turn, you take one action. Some actions are always available, like taking 1 coin. Some actions need a character, like Duke taking 3 coins or Assassin attacking someone. The twist is: you do not have to actually own the character to claim the action. You can bluff.

Staff mindset: Explain it as “poker face in 15 minutes”. Guests do not need heavy strategy knowledge, but they should enjoy bluffing and calling people out.

3. Components to check before giving the game

  • 15 character cards: 3 each of Duke, Assassin, Captain, Ambassador, and Contessa.
  • Coins / money tokens.
  • Player aid cards, if included. These are very useful for new players.
  • Rulebook, if guests want to double-check.
Cafe care: Count the 15 character cards before and after. Coup is tiny, so one missing card can spoil the whole game.

Best pitch to guests

“This is a quick bluffing game. You have two secret cards, but you can pretend to be anyone. Others can challenge you. If they catch your lie, you lose a card. Last player alive wins.”

4. Setup

  1. Shuffle all 15 character cards face down.
  2. Deal 2 cards face down to each player. Players may look at their own cards but must keep them secret.
  3. Put the remaining cards face down in the centre. This is the Court deck.
  4. Give each player 2 coins. In a 2-player game, the starting player gets only 1 coin.
  5. Choose a starting player. Turns go clockwise.
Important wording: Face-down cards are “influence”. Revealed cards are lost influence. A player with no face-down cards is eliminated.

5. Goal of the game

Be the last player with at least one face-down influence card. There is no second place. Once you are out, you can watch, laugh, and maybe remind people of their lies.

6. Turn structure

  1. The active player announces one action.
  2. Other players may challenge, if the action claims a character.
  3. Other players may block, if the action can be blocked.
  4. If nobody successfully stops it, the action happens.
  5. Then the next player takes a turn.
Simple staff line: “Say what you are doing. Give everyone a second to challenge or block. Then resolve it.”

9. Challenges

A challenge means: “I don’t believe you have that character.” Any player can challenge a character claim.

  1. Player claims a character action or block.
  2. Another player challenges.
  3. The challenged player must prove it by revealing the matching character, or admit they do not have it.
  4. If they have it: they reveal it, shuffle it into the Court deck, draw a replacement card, and the challenger loses 1 influence.
  5. If they do not have it: the challenged player loses 1 influence. Their action or block fails.
Key point: The winner of a challenge keeps two hidden cards if they still had two. They reveal the matching card only briefly, then replace it from the deck.

10. Blocks

A block is when a player says, “Your action does not work because I have this character.” Blocks are also character claims, so they can be challenged.

ActionWho can block?
Foreign AidDuke
AssassinationContessa, only the target can block
StealCaptain or Ambassador, usually only the target blocks
Easy teach: “You can challenge both actions and blocks, because both may be lies.”

11. Losing influence

  • When you lose influence, you choose one of your own face-down cards and reveal it.
  • Revealed cards stay face up in front of you. They do not go back into the deck.
  • You cannot use a revealed character anymore.
  • If both your cards are revealed, you are out of the game.
Staff tip: New players often ask, “Who chooses which card I lose?” Answer: the player losing influence chooses.

12. Example round

  1. Asha says, “I am the Duke. I take 3 coins.”
  2. Basil thinks she is lying and says, “Challenge.”
  3. Asha reveals Duke. She was telling the truth.
  4. Asha shuffles Duke into the deck and draws a replacement card.
  5. Basil loses one influence and reveals one of his two cards.

13. Another example: assassination

  1. Dev pays 3 coins and says, “Assassin on Nila.”
  2. Anyone may challenge whether Dev has Assassin.
  3. If no challenge, Nila may say, “I block with Contessa.”
  4. Now anyone may challenge Nila’s Contessa claim.
  5. If Nila’s block survives, she does not lose influence. If the block fails, she loses one influence.

14. Staff teaching script: 2-minute version

“Everyone gets two secret cards. These are your lives. On your turn, take one action. Some actions are safe: Income gives 1 coin, Foreign Aid gives 2 coins, and Coup costs 7 to make someone lose a card. Other actions need characters, like Duke takes 3, Assassin attacks, Captain steals, Ambassador swaps cards, and Contessa blocks assassination. But you can lie. If someone thinks you’re lying, they can challenge. Whoever is wrong loses a card. Last player with a hidden card wins.”

15. Common guest questions

Can I claim a character I do not have?

Yes. That is the main fun of Coup. But if someone challenges you and you cannot show that character, you lose influence.

Can I show my cards to prove something without a challenge?

No. Cards should stay secret unless a rule forces a reveal.

Can multiple people challenge at once?

Usually the first clear challenge is resolved. Staff can say, “One challenge at a time.”

Can I block a Coup?

No. Coup cannot be blocked or challenged. If someone pays 7, the target loses one influence.

Can I take Foreign Aid if someone has Duke revealed?

A revealed Duke is dead and cannot block. Only a player claiming a hidden Duke can block.

Can I steal if the target has only 1 coin?

Yes. You take what they have, up to 2 coins.

Can I assassinate with exactly 3 coins?

Yes. Pay all 3 coins, then resolve the assassination.

What if the Assassin is blocked by Contessa?

The 3 coins are still paid. The attack just does not make the target lose influence.

Can eliminated players talk?

For cafe fun, light jokes are okay, but they should not reveal hidden information or coach players seriously.

16. Common mistakes to watch for

  • Players forgetting that 10+ coins means they must Coup.
  • Players trying to block Coup. Coup cannot be blocked.
  • Players thinking they must actually have a character to claim it. They can bluff.
  • Players revealing cards casually. Remind them cards stay secret.
  • Players returning lost influence to the deck. Lost influence stays face up.
  • Players forgetting to replace a proven card after winning a challenge.

17. Best player count

Best: 4-6 players. More bluffing, more table talk, more funny accusations.

Okay: 3 players. Fast but slightly sharper.

2 players: Works, but feels more tactical and less social.

18. Cafe facilitation tips

  • Stay nearby for the first 2 rounds.
  • Remind guests to pause for challenges and blocks.
  • Give each player a reference card if available.
  • Encourage dramatic accusations; that is the vibe.

19. Who will enjoy Coup?

  • People who like bluffing, poker, Werewolf, Mafia, or Avalon.
  • Groups who want a quick “one more round” game.
  • Players who enjoy teasing friends and reading faces.

9. Challenges

Challenge എന്നത്: “നിനക്ക് ആ character ഉണ്ടെന്ന് ഞാൻ വിശ്വസിക്കുന്നില്ല” എന്ന് പറയുന്നതാണ്. Character claim ചെയ്താൽ ആരും challenge ചെയ്യാം.

  1. ഒരു player character action അല്ലെങ്കിൽ block claim ചെയ്യുന്നു.
  2. മറ്റൊരു player challenge ചെയ്യുന്നു.
  3. Challenged player matching character കാണിച്ച് prove ചെയ്യണം, അല്ലെങ്കിൽ card ഇല്ലെന്ന് സമ്മതിക്കണം.
  4. Matching character ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ: ആ card കാണിച്ച് Court deck-ൽ shuffle ചെയ്ത് replacement card draw ചെയ്യും. Challenger 1 influence lose ചെയ്യും.
  5. Matching character ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ: challenged player 1 influence lose ചെയ്യും. Action/block fail ചെയ്യും.
Key point: Challenge ജയിച്ച player matching card brief ആയി reveal ചെയ്ത് deck-ൽ shuffle ചെയ്ത് പുതിയ card എടുക്കും.

Hidden card ഉള്ള അവസാന player wins.”

15. Common guest questions

കൈയിൽ ഇല്ലാത്ത character claim ചെയ്യാമോ?

അതെ. അതാണ് Coup-ന്റെ main fun. പക്ഷേ challenge വന്നിട്ട് prove ചെയ്യാൻ പറ്റിയില്ലെങ്കിൽ influence lose ചെയ്യും.

Challenge ഇല്ലാതെ cards കാണിച്ച് prove ചെയ്യാമോ?

ഇല്ല. Rule force ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ മാത്രം card reveal ചെയ്യുക.

ഒരേ സമയം പലരും challenge ചെയ്താൽ?

ആദ്യം clear ആയി challenge പറഞ്ഞ player-നെ resolve ചെയ്യുക. Staff പറയാം: “One challenge at a time.”

Coup block ചെയ്യാമോ?

ഇല്ല. Coup block ചെയ്യാനും challenge ചെയ്യാനും പറ്റില്ല. 7 coins pay ചെയ്താൽ target 1 influence lose ചെയ്യും.

ഒരു player-ന്റെ Duke already reveal ആയാൽ അവർക്ക് Foreign Aid block ചെയ്യാമോ?

ഇല്ല. Revealed Duke dead ആണ്. Hidden Duke claim ചെയ്യുന്ന player-ന് മാത്രമേ block ചെയ്യാൻ പറ്റൂ.

Target-ന് 1 coin മാത്രം ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ steal ചെയ്യാമോ?

അതെ. Available coins എടുക്കാം, maximum 2 coins വരെ.

Exactly 3 coins ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ assassinate ചെയ്യാമോ?

അതെ. 3 coins മുഴുവൻ pay ചെയ്ത് assassination resolve ചെയ്യാം.

Assassin Contessa കൊണ്ട് block ചെയ്താൽ 3 coins തിരികെ കിട്ടുമോ?

ഇല്ല. 3 coins paid ആയി. Target influence lose ചെയ്യില്ല.

Out ആയ players സംസാരിക്കാമോ?

Light jokes okay. പക്ഷേ hidden information പറയുകയോ active players-നെ serious ആയി coach ചെയ്യുകയോ വേണ്ട.

16. Staff ശ്രദ്ധിക്കേണ്ട common mistakes

  • 10+ coins ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ Coup mandatory ആണെന്ന് players മറക്കും.
  • Coup block ചെയ്യാൻ ശ്രമിക്കും. Coup block ചെയ്യാൻ പറ്റില്ല.
  • Character action claim ചെയ്യാൻ ആ card കൈയിൽ വേണം എന്ന് കരുതും. Bluff ചെയ്യാം.
  • Cards casually reveal ചെയ്യും. Cards secret ആയിരിക്കണം.
  • Lost influence deck-ലേക്ക് return ചെയ്യും. Lost influence face up ആയി stays.
  • Challenge ജയിച്ച player proven card replace ചെയ്യാൻ മറക്കും.

17. Best player count

Best: 4-6 players. Bluffing, table talk, accusations എല്ലാം കൂടുതൽ fun.

Okay: 3 players. Fast ആണ്, പക്ഷേ sharper feel.

2 players: Works, but social bluffing കുറച്ച് tactical feel കൂടുതൽ.

18. Cafe facilitation tips

  • ആദ്യ 2 rounds staff അടുത്ത് ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ നല്ലത്.
  • Challenge/block ഉണ്ടോ എന്ന് wait ചെയ്യാൻ remind ചെയ്യുക.
  • Player aid cards ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ ഓരോരുത്തർക്കും കൊടുക്കുക.
  • Dramatic accusations encourage ചെയ്യുക. അതാണ് vibe.

19. Coup ആര്ക്കാണ് ഇഷ്ടമാവുക?

  • Bluffing, poker, Werewolf, Mafia, Avalon ഇഷ്ടമുള്ളവർക്ക്.
  • Quick “one more round” game വേണമെന്ന groups-ന്.
  • Friends-നെ tease ചെയ്യാനും face read ചെയ്യാനും ഇഷ്ടമുള്ള players-ന്.
